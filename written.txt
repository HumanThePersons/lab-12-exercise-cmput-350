Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
As circles move the bounding boxes stay valid but they stretch and overlap progressively
more and more, which means the BVH will get less efficient over time as the boxes move.
The boxes are drawn based on the circles spawn position, and they just re-adjust based
on that original position.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The tree gets organized around the current position of the circles - not the spawn position
like in TODO 3. The cost is higher, but the boxes don't get skewed over time and don't lose
the BVH efficiency.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
Circles are drawn based on their center point, within the window bounds, but if a circle is
drawn near an edge, its radius could still overlap the edge and be cutoff. The fix was to
determine radius first, and then make the range within which to put the center of the circle
have an offset on each side of the size of the radius.
